# Reading list and evidence ledger

This list favors primary research and maintainers. Revisit it quarterly because the capability frontier is moving quickly.

## Capability and time horizon

### METR: Task-Completion Time Horizons of Frontier AI Models

https://metr.org/time-horizons/

Use for: the current definition and measurements of 50%/80% task horizons.  
Caution: primarily self-contained software/ML/cybersecurity tasks; estimates above 16 hours are currently unreliable.

### METR: Clarifying limitations of time horizon

https://metr.org/notes/2026-01-22-time-horizon-limitations/

Use for: correct interpretation, domain differences, reliability thresholds and benchmark-to-reality caveats.

### METR: Time Horizon 1.1

https://metr.org/blog/2026-1-29-time-horizon-1-1/

Use for: suite changes, 228-task methodology and current trend discussion.

### SWE-bench Verified

https://www.swebench.com/verified

Use for: bounded issue-resolution capability.  
Caution: leaderboard performance does not establish production ownership, security or behavior on ambiguous work.

## Real-world productivity

### METR: Developer productivity experiment update

https://metr.org/blog/2026-02-24-uplift-update/

Use for: why the early-2025 19% slowdown result is outdated and why newer causal estimates are difficult.

### METR: Early-2026 technical worker survey

https://metr.org/blog/2026-05-11-ai-usage-survey/

Use for: perceived value versus speed and the limitations of self-report.

### METR: Coding transcript analysis

https://metr.org/notes/2026-02-17-exploratory-transcript-analysis-for-estimating-time-savings-from-coding-agents/

Use for: concurrency, selected-task savings and why they are an upper bound on overall uplift.

### Google DORA 2025

https://dora.dev/research/2025/dora-report/

Use for: organizational capabilities and the "AI as amplifier" model.

## Work patterns and expertise

### Anthropic Economic Index: AI's impact on software development

https://www.anthropic.com/research/impact-software-development

Use for: automation versus augmentation and early adoption patterns.  
Caution: vendor data from one product and selected users.

### Anthropic: Agentic coding and persistent returns to expertise

https://www.anthropic.com/research/claude-code-expertise

Use for: division between planning and execution, domain expertise and task-value trends.  
Caution: observational vendor study, not a causal labor-market result.

## How to read claims

For every result, ask:

1. Is this a benchmark, randomized experiment, survey or usage study?
2. What task distribution and users were selected?
3. Is the metric speed, output, quality or economic value?
4. What reliability threshold is reported?
5. Can the result transfer to high-context production work?
6. Is the source a vendor with product-selection effects?
7. What observation would falsify the conclusion on my own work?
